## 📚 Educational Content

### **Kinyarwanda Greetings Covered**
1. **Muraho** - Formal hello with cultural context
2. **Bite** - Informal greeting for friends/family
3. **Mwaramutse** - Morning greeting (until 10 AM)
4. **Mwiriwe** - Afternoon/evening greeting
5. **Ijoro ryiza** - Good night
6. **Murakoze** - Formal thank you
7. **Urakoze** - Informal thank you

- **Pronunciation Guides**: IPA-style phonetic spelling
